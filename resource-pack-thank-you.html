What’s Included?

Your free resource pack contains a collection of practical templates, printable checklists and planning guides designed to support UK beekeepers throughout the year.

  • Apiary Risk Assessment Template
  • Bee Health Checker Printable Checklist
  • PPE for Varroa Treatments
  • Chemical Varroa Treatments Overview
  • Non-Chemical Varroa Methods
  • Landowner–Beekeeper Agreement Template
  • Year in the Apiary Monthly Seasonal Strategy
  • Year in the Apiary Beekeeping Calendar

What To Do Next

Save the resource pack somewhere easy to find, print the documents you need, and keep them with your hive records or inspection folder.

These resources are intended to support good beekeeping practice, but they do not replace official advice where notifiable disease or serious colony health problems are suspected.
