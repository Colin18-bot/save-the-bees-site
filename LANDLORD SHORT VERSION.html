2) Purpose

This agreement allows the Beekeeper to place and manage up to [X] honey bee colonies at the Apiary location for beekeeping and honey bee welfare.

3) Key terms (summary)

  • The bees and beekeeping equipment remain the Beekeeper’s property.
  • The Beekeeper is responsible for routine management, disease control, and safe working practices.
  • The Landowner grants reasonable access subject to agreed routes/times and site rules.
  • The Beekeeper will hold appropriate public liability insurance and provide evidence on request.
  • Either party may end this agreement with [X days/weeks] written notice (unless urgent safety concerns require immediate termination).

4) Safety, nuisance & neighbours

  • The Beekeeper will site colonies to minimise nuisance (flight paths, water provision, calm handling).
  • The Beekeeper will keep the apiary tidy and remove waste, old equipment, and hazards.
  • The Landowner will inform the Beekeeper of planned site activities that could affect the bees (e.g., spraying, livestock movements, events).
