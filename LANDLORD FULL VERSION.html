2) Purpose & scope

The Landowner permits the Beekeeper to place and manage honey bee colonies at the site for beekeeping and honey bee welfare, subject to the terms below.

3) Beekeeper responsibilities

  1. Competent management: Maintain colonies appropriately, monitor welfare, and respond to problems promptly.
  2. Varroa/disease control: Follow current UK guidance and product instructions; keep suitable treatment records.
  3. Insurance: Maintain public liability insurance appropriate for beekeeping and provide evidence upon request.
  4. Site care: Respect fences, livestock, crops, and infrastructure; keep the apiary neat and remove waste.
  5. Access rules: Use only agreed routes; close gates; avoid access in conditions likely to cause damage unless emergency.
  6. No alterations: Do not remove/prune trees, shrubs, or plants without written permission.
  7. Registration: Register colonies on BeeBase / NBU (or relevant national system) where applicable.

4) Landowner responsibilities

  1. Access: Allow reasonable access for inspections and management at mutually agreed times.
  2. Site changes: Notify the Beekeeper of relevant works/activities (e.g., mowing near apiary, spraying, events, livestock movements).
  3. Third parties: Where practical, avoid directing visitors/contractors into the apiary area.

6) Biosecurity & records

  • Keep records of treatments/medicines where applicable (date, product, hive, dose, notes, withdrawals).
  • Use sensible biosecurity: clean tools, avoid moving risky combs between sites, manage robbing risks.
  • If a notifiable disease is suspected, follow official processes and inform the Landowner where relevant.

7) Termination & removal

Immediate termination: The Landowner may require immediate removal where there is a serious safety issue, unlawful activity, or significant breach of site rules.
