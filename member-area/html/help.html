Help & Guide

Getting Started

Welcome to the HiveTag Member Area. This is your personalised beekeeping hub for managing apiaries, hives, inspections, logs, and tasks.

To begin, use the “Setup Checklist” on your Dashboard to add your first apiary and hive.

Navigation Overview

  • Dashboard: View quick stats, upcoming tasks, and weather.
  • Apiaries: Manage apiary locations and view associated hives.
  • Hives: View all hives by apiary, inspect hives, or clone/archive them.
  • Inspections: Log and review hive inspections with PDF export.
  • To-Do: Schedule tasks like feeding, harvesting, or treatments.
  • Hive Logbook: Record detailed activity logs like feeding, treatments, and requeening.
  • Calendar: See all scheduled inspections and tasks by date.
  • Weather: Get a 3-day forecast by apiary location to plan work efficiently.
  • Groups: Share access and collaborate with other beekeepers (if enabled).
  • Settings: Update your profile or delete your account.

Managing Apiaries

Navigate to Apiaries to add, edit, or delete apiary locations. You can place a pin on the map, upload a photo, and choose a default apiary.

Working with Hives

Each apiary can hold multiple hives. From the Hives page, you can:

  • Filter hives by apiary
  • Inspect a hive
  • Edit or clone an existing hive
  • Archive or permanently delete a hive

Logging Inspections

Use the Inspections page to record detailed inspection notes, conditions, and actions taken. Each record includes weather and can be exported as a PDF.

Using the To-Do and Logbook Tools

To-Do: Create upcoming tasks with due dates and link them to apiaries or hives.

Hive Logbook: Document key events like feeding, treatments, and queen marking, with photos and linked inspections.

Weather & Calendar

The Weather tab provides a forecast for each apiary to help you plan inspections and fieldwork. The Calendar shows all upcoming and past actions in one view.
