HiveTag Association Partner Programme

Supporting beekeeping associations and their members

HiveTag works with beekeeping associations to give members access to practical digital record-keeping tools, exclusive Premium discounts and opportunities to help shape future development.

Become an Association Partner Explore HiveTag
Home › HiveTag › Association Partner Programme

Benefits for your members

  • Exclusive 20% discount on HiveTag Premium
  • Apiary, hive and inspection record keeping
  • Hive Health and colony history
  • Ongoing Queen Records and queen history
  • Reports, exports, tasks, calendar and Premium tools

Benefits for the association

  • Up to three complimentary Premium accounts
  • Early involvement in future feature development
  • No fee, sales target or requirement to endorse HiveTag

How the partnership works

Each participating association receives its own member offer for HiveTag Premium. The association can share the offer with its members through its usual newsletter, website, social media or meetings.

There is no requirement for an association to formally endorse HiveTag. We simply ask associations to take a look at the service and, if they feel it could benefit their members, consider making them aware of the offer.

Founding Association Partners

We are currently inviting a small number of UK beekeeping associations to become our first Association Partners. These founding partners will have an early opportunity to provide feedback and help influence future HiveTag development.

There is no charge to join the HiveTag Association Partner Programme and no sales target or obligation to endorse HiveTag.

If your association would like to take part, contact us and we can arrange your association member offer and complimentary Premium accounts.
