HiveTag – Your Beekeeping Records in One Place

HiveTag is the BeezKnees members app for keeping track of your apiaries, hives, inspections, tasks and beekeeping costs. It’s built for real beekeepers who want simple, practical records – not complex software.

Use it on desktop, tablet or mobile through your browser. There’s nothing to install, and your data stays in sync wherever you log in.

  • Record hive inspections while you’re at the apiary
  • See what each hive needs at a glance
  • Keep a running log of tasks, feeding and observations
  • Track inventory, sales and expenses for your beekeeping

Start on the Free plan and upgrade later if you need more.

Open Members Area    View Plans & Features

What HiveTag Helps You With

Stay Organised in the Apiary

Log every inspection with notes on brood, stores, queen status, temperament and actions taken. Quickly see the last visit for each hive so you don’t miss important follow-ups.

Know What Each Hive Needs

Link tasks and to-dos to specific apiaries and hives. From feeding and treatments to equipment changes, HiveTag keeps a clear list of what needs doing and where.

Keep on Top of Costs

Track inventory items, expenses and sales so you can see what your beekeeping is really costing – or earning. Helpful whether you sell a few jars to friends or run a small sideline.

How HiveTag Fits Into Your Beekeeping

HiveTag is designed to sit alongside your real hive work:

  1. Create your account and choose the Free plan.
  2. Add your apiaries and hives so each colony has a clear home in the app.
  3. Record inspections and tasks during or just after your visits, so nothing is forgotten.
  4. Log inventory, sales and expenses to get a better picture of your beekeeping costs.

Free Plan vs Premium – What’s Included?

HiveTag is built so you can start small and grow into more features when you need them. Here’s a simple overview in plain language:

Apiaries & Hives

Free plan: Ideal for a single apiary and a handful of hives.

Premium plan: Better if you have multiple apiaries or your hive numbers are growing and you want more headroom.

Inspections, Logbook & Tasks

Free plan: Core inspection records, notes and basic task lists so you can keep on top of key jobs.

Premium plan: Same simple layout but more space and flexibility, especially useful if you manage many colonies or helpers.

Inventory, Sales & Expenses

Free plan: Great for tracking small-scale costs and the odd jar of honey sold to friends or neighbours.

Premium plan: More useful if you sell regularly, run a sideline or want a clearer picture of profit and loss.

You can stay on the Free plan for as long as you like. If you grow your beekeeping and want more headroom, upgrading to Premium is done securely through Stripe inside the app.

Built by a Beekeeper, Not a Software Company

HiveTag grows out of the same thinking as the main BeezKnees website: practical, beginner-friendly and focused on what’s genuinely useful in the apiary. It isn’t about ticking boxes for enterprise features – it exists to help you keep better bees.

Whether you’re running a single hive at the end of the garden or starting a small out-apiary, HiveTag is there to support your decision making, not replace it. The real work is still done by you – and the bees.
