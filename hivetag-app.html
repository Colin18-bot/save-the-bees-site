Built around the way you actually work in the apiary

HiveTag focuses on a few things done well – not endless menus and settings. It’s designed to sit alongside your hive tool and smoker, not replace them.

Stay organised at each hive

Log inspections with notes on brood, stores, queen status, temperament and actions taken. Quickly see when you last visited each hive so you don’t miss important follow-ups.

Know what needs doing – and where

Link tasks and to-dos to apiaries and hives. From feeding and treatments to equipment changes, HiveTag keeps a clear list of what needs doing and where.

Keep on top of costs

Track inventory items, expenses and sales so you can see what your beekeeping is really costing – or earning. Helpful whether you sell a few jars to friends or run a small sideline.

How HiveTag fits into your season

The app is designed to be simple enough that you’ll actually use it after a long afternoon in the bees.

1
Create your account on the Free plan. Add your name and email, log in from any device and you’re ready to go.
2
Add your apiaries and hives. Give each colony a clear home in the app so you can see which hive is where and what’s in it.
3
Record inspections and jobs as you go. Log checks, feeding, treatments and observations while you’re at the hive – or when you get back indoors.
4
Track inventory, sales and expenses. Keep an eye on where your money is going – from jars and foundation to honey sales.
