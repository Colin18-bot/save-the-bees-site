For real beekeepers

Keep your hive records, tasks and costs together – without complicated software.

HiveTag is a simple web app for hobby and small-scale beekeepers. Record inspections, keep track of what each hive needs and see where your beekeeping money is going over the season.

It runs in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop – nothing to install, just log in and go, whether you keep bees in Cardiff, the Vale of Glamorgan or elsewhere in the UK.

Not sure what you’re looking at during an inspection? HiveTag includes a Colony Health Check (bee disease helper) that guides you through common symptoms so you can record what you observe and decide what to check next. It’s designed to support good note-taking, not replace official guidance or bee inspector advice.

HiveTag works fully without NFC for simple digital record-keeping. If you choose to upgrade to the Premium plan, you can also use optional NFC hive tags to tap a hive and open its record instantly — ideal for busy apiary days and wet gloves. NFC features require a compatible device and separately purchased tags.

Built around the way you actually work in the apiary

HiveTag focuses on a few things done well – not endless menus and settings. It’s designed to sit alongside your hive tool and smoker, not replace them.

Stay organised at each hive

Log inspections with notes on brood, stores, queen status, temperament and actions taken. Quickly see when you last visited each hive so you don’t miss important follow-ups or seasonal jobs.

Know what needs doing – and where

Link tasks and to-dos to apiaries and hives. From feeding and treatments to equipment changes, HiveTag keeps a clear list of what needs doing and where, so you can plan your next visit more easily.

Keep on top of costs

Track inventory items, expenses and sales so you can see what your beekeeping is really costing – or earning. Helpful whether you sell a few jars to friends or run a small sideline.

Colony Health Check (bee disease helper)

Use the Colony Health Check to guide your observations during inspections. It helps you note common signs and prompts you to record what you see so you can make better follow-up decisions. It is not a diagnosis tool and it does not replace official advice for notifiable diseases.

Questions beekeepers often ask

A few common questions from new users. If you’re still unsure whether HiveTag is right for you, you can always start on the Free plan and try it alongside your current notebook.

Do I need any special hardware to use HiveTag?

No. HiveTag runs in a web browser on most modern phones, tablets and laptops. HiveTag works fully without NFC. Optional NFC hive tags are available on the Premium plan for quicker access to hive records with a tap.

Does HiveTag include a bee disease checker?

Yes. HiveTag includes a Colony Health Check (bee disease helper) that guides you through common symptoms so you can record what you observe during inspections. It is not a diagnosis tool and it does not replace official advice for notifiable diseases.

Will HiveTag work if I keep bees outside South Wales?

Yes. Although it was built by a beekeeper in South Wales and tested around Cardiff and the Vale of Glamorgan, HiveTag is designed for hobbyist beekeepers anywhere in the UK.

Is my data tied to one device?

No. Your records are stored securely on our managed database. You can log in from any device with a browser and internet connection, so you can check hive notes at home or in the apiary.

Can I stop using HiveTag if it’s not for me?

Of course. You can export or copy your key records at any time and cancel a paid plan if you decide it’s not right for the way you keep bees.
