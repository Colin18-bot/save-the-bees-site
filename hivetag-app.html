HiveTag: a UK beekeeping app for hive inspections, apiary records and tasks

HiveTag is a UK beekeeping records app designed to replace paper hive inspection sheets, notebooks and scattered spreadsheets. It brings apiaries, hives, inspections, tasks, queen histories, colony health and costs together in one simple place.

HiveTag works fully without NFC for simple digital record-keeping. If you choose to upgrade to the Premium plan, you can also use optional NFC hive tags to tap a hive and open its record instantly — ideal for busy apiary days and wet gloves. NFC features require a compatible device and separately purchased tags.

Why Use a Beekeeping App?

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Many beekeepers start with notebooks, paper inspection sheets or spreadsheets. These can work well at first, but become difficult to manage as colonies, apiaries and inspection records grow.

A dedicated beekeeping app allows you to keep all hive records in one place, including inspections, apiaries, hive histories, queen records, tasks, treatments, feeding records and seasonal notes.

HiveTag was built specifically as a UK beekeeping records app, helping hobby and small-scale beekeepers move from paper records to simple digital record keeping.

Whether you are looking for a hive inspection app, apiary management software, a beekeeping logbook app or a better way to organise colony records, HiveTag brings everything together in one place.

Tired of Paper Hive Records?

Many beekeepers start with notebooks, scraps of paper or printed inspection sheets. The problem is that records become difficult to find when you need them most.

HiveTag keeps inspections, hive histories, apiaries, tasks and seasonal records together so you can quickly see what happened last week, last month or last season.

No more trying to remember whether a colony was queenless, when it was fed, when treatment was applied or which hive needed a follow-up inspection.

Asian Hornet Identification and Reporting Tools – Included Free

HiveTag includes an Asian Hornet Centre for all users on both the Free and Premium plans. It is designed to help UK beekeepers identify the Yellow-legged Asian Hornet (Vespa velutina), compare commonly confused species and keep a record of suspected sightings.

If you see something suspicious, you can create an observation, add photographs, review identification features and compare the insect with common lookalikes. HiveTag also reminds you when a suspected sighting still needs to be reported through the official reporting service.

Observations saved in HiveTag are private records and do not themselves constitute an official Asian Hornet report. Suspected sightings should still be submitted through the official UK reporting channels.

Identify Asian Hornets

Review the main identification features of the Yellow-legged Asian Hornet and use clear visual guidance while examining a suspected sighting.

Compare Common Lookalikes

Compare Asian Hornets with insects that are commonly mistaken for them, including the European Hornet, common wasp, giant woodwasp and hornet-mimic hoverfly.

Record Your Observations

Keep suspected sightings together in My Observations so photographs and observation details can be reviewed again when needed.

Official Reporting Reminder

HiveTag clearly shows when a suspected Asian Hornet observation still needs to be submitted through the official reporting service.

For detailed identification, safety advice and official reporting information, see our Asian Hornet UK identification and reporting guide. Do not disturb a suspected nest or attempt nest removal yourself.

Keep Complete Queen Records and Queen Histories

Queen information can become difficult to follow when colonies are split, requeened or moved between hives. HiveTag Premium includes dedicated Queen Records so the story of each queen does not disappear into old inspection notes.

Record queen introductions, status changes, splits, transfers and requeening events, then review the continuing history alongside the colony. This creates a clearer digital queen history when you need to understand what happened earlier in the season or across several generations of queens.

Build a continuing queen history

Keep previous and current queen information together instead of relying on scattered notebook entries or individual inspection notes.

Record important queen events

Log introductions, queen status changes, requeening and other key events so you can see when and why the colony changed.

Follow splits and transfers

When a queen moves with a split or is transferred to another hive, the history can follow the queen while the original colony record remains historically accurate.

Review before opening the hive

Check the queen record alongside recent colony information before an inspection so you know what you are expecting to find.

See Colony Health Trends with Hive Health

A single inspection only shows what you saw on one day. Hive Health is designed to help Premium users look across their recorded colony information and see how the picture is changing over time.

HiveTag presents explainable health scores, trends, risks and recommended actions, together with a dashboard overview and colony timeline. The aim is to make your own records easier to interpret and help you decide what deserves attention at the next inspection.

Explainable health scores

Keep the colony timeline in view

Hive Health supports record keeping and beekeeper decision making; it is not a diagnosis service. For practical health information, use the Bee Health and Varroa Management guides.

Questions beekeepers often ask

Is the HiveTag Asian Hornet Centre free?

Yes. The Asian Hornet Centre is available to both Free and Premium HiveTag users. It includes identification guidance, lookalike comparisons, observations, photographs and links to official reporting information. Saving an observation in HiveTag does not itself submit an official Asian Hornet report.

Do I need any special hardware to use HiveTag?

No. HiveTag runs in a web browser on most modern phones, tablets and laptops. Optional NFC hive tags are available on the Premium plan.

Is HiveTag suitable for beginner beekeepers?

Yes. HiveTag is designed to make hive record keeping straightforward for beginners as well as more experienced beekeepers. The Free plan lets you start with one apiary and two hives, record inspections and manage tasks without needing specialist equipment or a paid subscription.

Does HiveTag include a bee disease checker?

Yes. HiveTag Premium includes a Colony Health Check that guides you through common symptoms so you can record what you observe during inspections.

Can HiveTag keep queen records and queen histories?

Yes. HiveTag Premium includes dedicated Queen Records so you can keep a continuing queen history, record introductions, splits, transfers, requeening and status changes, and retain that history as colonies change over time.

How does HiveTag help me monitor colony health?

HiveTag Premium includes Hive Health, which uses your recorded inspection information to show explainable health scores, trends, risks and recommended actions over time. It supports beekeeper decision making and does not replace diagnosis or professional advice.

Does HiveTag include seasonal beekeeping guidance?

Yes. HiveTag Premium includes the Year in the Apiary seasonal guide, with monthly UK beekeeping priorities, risks, infographic guides and one-click task creation.

Will HiveTag work outside South Wales?

Yes. Although it was built by a beekeeper in South Wales, HiveTag is designed for hobby and small-scale beekeepers anywhere in the UK.

Is my data tied to one device?

No. You can log in from any device with a browser and internet connection, so you can check hive notes at home or in the apiary.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can stay on the Free plan for as long as you like and upgrade securely through Stripe later if your beekeeping grows.

Can I stop using HiveTag if it's not for me?

Of course. You can export or copy your key records at any time and cancel a paid plan if you decide it's not right for the way you keep bees.
