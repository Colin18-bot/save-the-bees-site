Free Beekeeping Downloads UK

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Download free UK beekeeping checklists, templates and printable PDF guides created to help beekeepers organise hive inspections, plan seasonal work, manage varroa, record medicines and keep clearer apiary records.

These BeezKnees resources are designed for practical use in the apiary, whether you keep one hive in the garden, manage several colonies, support a beginners' group or want simple printable records to keep alongside your digital notes.

What's Included?

The downloads on this page cover the main areas where written records and printable references are useful for UK beekeepers: health checks, varroa planning, apiary safety, landowner arrangements, seasonal jobs and medicine records.

  • Bee Health Checker: printable checklist for symptoms, confirming checks, severity and red-flag actions.
  • Varroa guides: chemical treatment overview, non-chemical methods and integrated pest management planning.
  • PPE notes: a practical reminder for safe treatment handling and preparation.
  • Veterinary medicine record: link to the official BeeBase/National Bee Unit record form.
  • Apiary risk assessment: template for site hazards, access, public safety and sensible controls.
  • Landowner agreement: useful for hives kept on another person's land.
  • Year in the Apiary planner: printable seasonal calendar for monthly beekeeping tasks.

Why Use Beekeeping Checklists and Templates?

Good record keeping helps you see patterns across the season. A single inspection note may not tell you much, but several weeks of notes can show whether a colony is building well, losing brood, running short of stores, becoming queenless or repeatedly showing signs of stress.

Printable beekeeping templates are useful because they encourage consistency. Instead of trying to remember what you saw at the hive, you can record important details such as brood pattern, queen status, food stores, temperament, disease concerns, varroa treatment dates and follow-up jobs.

For UK beekeepers, records can also support better decisions around varroa treatments, veterinary medicines, apiary safety, landowner communication and seasonal planning. They do not replace official advice, but they can help you stay organised and act earlier when something changes.

Bee Health Checker UK – Printable Checklist

The Bee Health Checker is a practical symptoms-to-actions resource for UK beekeepers. It is designed to help you think through visible colony signs, possible causes and sensible next steps during or after an inspection.

It includes confirming checks, severity notes and mini callouts for situations where extra caution is needed, especially where foulbrood or other serious disease may be a possibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these beekeeping downloads free?

Yes. The downloads on this page are free to use. Some are provided as PDFs for printing, and some are provided as Word documents so you can adapt them.

Are these resources suitable for beginner beekeepers?

Yes. Many of the checklists and templates are designed to help beginner beekeepers organise inspections, understand common hive problems and keep better records.

Are the downloads written for UK beekeepers?

Yes. The resources are written with UK beekeeping practice in mind, including UK seasonal planning, apiary records, varroa management and links to relevant UK sources where appropriate.

Can I edit the templates?

Where a Word version is provided, you can adapt the template for your own apiary, beekeeping association, training group or landowner arrangement.

Do these downloads replace official bee health advice?

No. The downloads are educational resources and practical record-keeping aids. If you suspect notifiable disease or a serious colony health issue, seek official advice.

Do I need a HiveTag account to use the downloads?

No. You can use the downloads without a HiveTag account. HiveTag is available if you prefer to keep your hive records digitally.
